# Requirements Discovery Session Guide

## Session objective

Use this session to confirm what the service actually needs before any formal pilot or build commitment.

This is not a clinical sign-off meeting and not a production-readiness meeting.

## Before the meeting

Send the pack to attendees 2-3 days before the session. Ask them to:
- Pre-read the sheets
- Start filling in answers where they can
- Flag anything they don't understand or that needs someone else to answer

## Best framing to use

Suggested wording:

> I reviewed your email and the documents you shared, and I have put what I understood into a structured set of sheets. I have prefilled the parts I think I understand so your team can confirm, correct, or add what is missing. My goal today is to reduce assumptions and make sure we know who owns which decisions.

## Meeting structure

1. Confirm session purpose (5 min)
2. Ask about scope boundary with the other vendor's work (5 min)
3. Review `01-current-understanding.csv` (15 min)
4. Review `02-clinical-validation.csv` (20 min)
5. Review `03-workflow-and-operations.csv` (10 min)
6. Review `04-technical-integration-ai-hosting.csv` (10 min)
7. Agree `05-decisions-and-owners.csv` (10 min)

## What to ask for by the end

- Confirmed first pilot service:
  - `Colposcopy`
  - `Gynaecology`
  - `Both`
- Named clinical owner for colposcopy validation
- Named clinical owner for gynaecology validation
- Named workflow or operational owner
- Named technical or integration contact
- Clarification on whether the first pilot should be:
  - structured rules only (no AI)
  - one-page summary with a suggested outcome for the clinician to confirm
  - AI-assisted grading
- Understanding of the boundary with the other vendor's work

## What not to do

- Do not position this as clinically complete
- Do not force hosting or AI-platform assumptions
- Do not try to settle unresolved governance issues yourself
- Do not overload the session with screen designs or implementation detail
- Do not assume the other vendor's scope -- ask

## What a good outcome looks like

- Major assumptions corrected
- Pilot scope narrowed
- Current systems and referral sources identified
- Missing data sources identified
- Decision owners assigned
- Boundary with the other vendor's work clarified
- Follow-up work split into:
  - clinical validation
  - operational workflow
  - technical and integration
